KAS Prelims Paper II: Quantitative Aptitude, Mental Ability & Comprehension (Q1–50)

50 questions · 50 minutes · +1 for each correct answer · −0.25 for each wrong answer (the negative-marking pattern in recent KEA notifications). The questions follow the quantitative and mental ability pattern of KAS Prelims Paper II (2017 and 2020): comprehension passages, data interpretation from tables, seating arrangement, logical facts, work, time and speed, percentages, ages, profit and loss, interest, series, coding, blood relations, directions, syllogisms, data sufficiency and decision making. They are original practice questions modelled on the syllabus and pattern, not copies of past papers.

Question palette: green = answered, red = visited but not answered, purple = marked for review, grey = not visited. Marked questions that have an answer are counted at submission.
